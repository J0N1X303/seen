About this story

This is a fictional creative prototype about persistent unwanted attention, what a group of friends notices and what someone who has already said no cannot know about their intentions. It also explores how a small intervention can matter. It is not intended to represent every woman’s experience, or to suggest that someone’s fears prove another person’s intentions.

This fiction has not yet received independent specialist or lived-experience review. Before publication, we intend to seek review of the narrative and safeguarding information. For practical bystander advice, visit Rape Crisis England & Wales.

Content note: persistent unwanted attention and uncertainty about personal safety. No assault is depicted.
